
# Hey there, I'm Songqi 👋

**Songqi Duan (段松岐)** · aka `zerostwo`

*Trying to understand biology, one cell (and one bug) at a time.*

I'm a PhD student at **City of Hope**, working in **Ma Lab**.

I spend my days somewhere between the wet lab and the terminal — exploring the immune system, analyzing single-cell data, building computational tools, and occasionally breaking things in the name of science.

> Biology is complicated. My code doesn't have to be.
> (Although sometimes it is.)

### 🧬 What keeps me busy

- 🔬 **Computational Immunology** — Exploring immune cell diversity, especially ILCs and NK cells.
- 🧬 **Single-cell & Spatial Omics** — Building cell atlases and mapping immune cells across tissues and diseases.
- 🧪 **Experimental Biology** — Studying how immune cells interact and respond to environmental signals.
- 💻 **Open Source** — Building tools, automating workflows, and making bioinformatics a little less painful.
- 🤖 **AI for Biology** — Exploring how machine learning can help us understand complex biological systems.

### ⚡ A few things about me

- I like turning complicated biological questions into computational problems.
- I believe a good visualization is worth a thousand lines of code.
- I open-source things, break things, and sometimes even fix them.
- My experiments have taught me that cells don't always read the protocol.
- I spend an unreasonable amount of time making UMAPs look pretty.
